Tools in action

Pick your ground crew

Your coding agents, at home on your desktop.

Your terminal.
Your desktop.
Same task.

Open a task in the terminal. Move it to another machine. Bring it into the Codex desktop app, or resume when you’re ready. Keep the conversation with the work.

Desktop handoff on macOS · Terminal workflows on macOS & Linux

Get t →Watch it in the terminal ↓
01 / Open

Start here.

t open my-project --codex

Open your agent in an isolated worktree, with your selected model.

02 / Another host

Move it there.

t beam my-project 1 --host mini

Move the task and its conversation to your other machine. Bring it home with t beam my-project 1 --from mini

03 / Desktop macOS

Open in the app.

t open my-project 1 --app

Continue the local Codex conversation with your preview and plan alongside it.

04 / Resume

Pick it back up.

t resume my-project 1

Return to a saved conversation after its terminal session ends.
